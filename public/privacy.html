Wolfram privacy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Wolfram collects nothing. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What stays on your device

  • Your training log: sessions, sets, weights and the classes you log.
  • Your settings: weekly targets, the times you can get to class, your equipment.
  • Your class list and any notes you add.

None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party data-collection code.

Backups

If you export a backup, Wolfram creates a file and hands it to you. Where it goes from there (a folder, a message, a cloud drive) is your choice. Deleting the app deletes its data, so keep a backup if you want one.

iPhone app permissions

  • Notifications: used only to tell you when a rest timer ends. They are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent over the internet.
  • Microphone: used only if you choose to record your own end-of-rest sound in Settings. The recording stays on your phone.

The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver the pages, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, under GitHub's privacy statement. Wolfram itself still stores your data only in your browser.

Questions

If you use the iPhone app through TestFlight, send feedback from the TestFlight app and it will reach the developer.
